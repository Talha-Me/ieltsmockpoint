What is the IELTS Reading test?

The IELTS Reading test checks how well you can read for gist, detail, opinion and argument. It lasts 60 minutes and contains 40 questions across 3 passages. In IELTS Academic, the passages come from books, journals, magazines and newspapers written for a general audience. Each correct answer earns one mark, and your raw score out of 40 is converted into a band from 0 to 9.

IELTS Reading Question Types and Tips for a Higher Band Score

Knowing each question type is the fastest way to improve your IELTS reading score. Use the free mock tests above to apply these strategies under real exam conditions.

True / False / Not Given and Yes / No / Not Given

These questions test whether a statement matches the passage. True or Yes means the text agrees, False or No means it contradicts the statement, and Not Given means the text does not say. Never use your own knowledge. Underline the keyword, find the matching sentence and compare the exact meaning, including words such as "all", "only", "often" and "never".

Matching headings

Read the first and last sentence of each paragraph to find its main idea, then match it to the heading. Remember that one heading is usually extra, and a heading should cover the whole paragraph, not just one detail. Do these questions after you have skimmed the passage once.

Multiple choice

Read the question first, locate the relevant section, then eliminate options that are clearly wrong. Distractors often repeat words from the text but change the meaning, so check the full idea instead of matching single words.

Sentence, summary, note and table completion

Check the word limit carefully, for example "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS", and write words exactly as they appear in the passage. Decide the grammar needed in the gap (noun, verb, adjective) before you search for the answer.

Matching information and matching features

For matching information, you must find which paragraph contains a given detail. Answers can appear in any order and a paragraph may be used more than once. For matching features, scan the passage for names and then read around them for the opinion or fact required.

Short-answer questions

Answer with words taken directly from the text, and follow the word limit. These questions follow the order of the passage, which makes them quick if you stay organised.

Time management tips for the 60-minute test

  • Aim for about 20 minutes per passage, and move on if one question is taking too long.
  • Skim first, then scan. Get the structure of the passage, then hunt for keywords and paraphrases.
  • Write answers as you go. There is no extra time to transfer answers at the end of the Reading test.
  • Leave nothing blank. There is no negative marking, so always make an educated guess.
  • Save Passage 3 for last in your practice routine, then build up to doing all three in one sitting.
  • Keep an error log of the question types you miss, and repeat them in passage-wise practice.

IELTS Reading Practice Test Guide: Format, Scoring and Study Plan

If you are searching for an IELTS reading test, a reading mock test free or the best way to practise IELTS reading online, this guide explains how the test works and how to use each free mock on this page to raise your band score.

IELTS Academic Reading vs General Training Reading

Both versions have 40 questions in 60 minutes and use the same band scale. IELTS Academic Reading has three long, analytical texts taken from books, journals, magazines and newspapers. IELTS General Training Reading uses shorter everyday texts such as notices, advertisements and work-related documents in the first sections, followed by a longer text. The Cambridge IELTS Academic reading tests on this page are ideal for Academic candidates, and the skimming, scanning and paraphrase skills you build also help General Training candidates.

How IELTS Reading is scored

Each correct answer is worth one mark, with no negative marking, so always answer every question. Spelling must be correct, and both UK and US spellings are accepted. Your raw score out of 40 is converted into a band from 0 to 9, and your instant result after each mock test shows exactly which answers were right or wrong so you can study your mistakes.

Reading skills tested in the IELTS Reading test

  • Skimming: getting the main idea and structure of a passage quickly.
  • Scanning: finding names, dates, numbers and keywords without reading every word.
  • Paraphrase recognition: matching a question to a sentence that uses different words with the same meaning.
  • Inference: understanding the writer's opinion, purpose and attitude.
  • Detailed reading: checking exact meaning for True / False / Not Given and multiple choice questions.

Common mistakes in the IELTS reading test

  • Spending too long on one hard question and running out of time on Passage 3.
  • Using outside knowledge instead of the information in the passage.
  • Confusing False with Not Given in True / False / Not Given questions.
  • Ignoring the word limit in completion questions.
  • Copying the wrong form of a word, or misspelling an answer taken from the text.
  • Matching a heading to one detail instead of the main idea of the paragraph.

A simple 4-week IELTS Reading practice plan

  • Week 1: take one full timed test to find your baseline, then study every mistake and list your weak question types.
  • Week 2: use passage-wise practice daily, focusing on the two question types that cost you the most marks.
  • Week 3: sit two full mock tests, aiming for 20 minutes per passage and finishing with time to check answers.
  • Week 4: repeat weak tests, work on vocabulary and synonyms, and take a final full mock in exam conditions.

Frequently asked questions about the free IELTS Reading mock test

Quick answers about our free IELTS reading mock tests and Cambridge IELTS reading practice.

Is this IELTS Reading mock test really free?

Yes. You can practise Cambridge IELTS Reading tests online on IELTS Mockpoint for free and get your score straight after you finish.

How long is the IELTS Reading test?

The IELTS Reading test lasts 60 minutes and has 40 questions across 3 passages. There is no extra time to transfer answers, so managing your time is important.

Can I practise one passage at a time?

Yes. Every test has a Practice mode where you choose Passage 1, Passage 2 or Passage 3 and work on your weak areas without sitting the full test.

Which Cambridge IELTS books are available?

Cambridge IELTS 12 to 21 are available, each with 4 full reading tests, which gives you 40 free IELTS reading mock tests in total.

How is my IELTS Reading band score calculated?

Each correct answer earns one mark out of 40. Your raw score is then converted to a band from 0 to 9. The conversion differs slightly between Academic and General Training.

What question types appear in IELTS Reading?

Common types include multiple choice, True/False/Not Given, Yes/No/Not Given, matching headings, matching information, sentence completion, summary completion and short answer questions.

How many correct answers do I need for Band 6, 7 or 8 in IELTS Academic Reading?

As a rough guide for IELTS Academic Reading, about 23 out of 40 gives Band 6, about 30 out of 40 gives Band 7 and about 35 out of 40 gives Band 8. Conversion tables can vary slightly between test versions.

What is the difference between IELTS Academic and General Training Reading?

Both versions have 40 questions in 60 minutes. Academic Reading uses three long texts from books, journals and magazines. General Training Reading uses shorter everyday texts in the early sections and a longer text in the final section, and its band score conversion is stricter.

How often should I take an IELTS reading mock test?

Most learners improve fastest by taking one full timed mock test per week and doing passage-wise practice on other days. Always review every wrong answer before you move to the next test.

How can I improve my IELTS Reading score quickly?

Practise under real timing, learn the rules of each question type, skim for keywords and paraphrases instead of reading every word, and keep an error log. Repeating weak question types in passage-wise practice usually gives the quickest gains.

Is this the same as the real computer-delivered IELTS Reading test?

The mock tests are built to feel like the on-screen exam, with a countdown timer, highlighting and note-taking tools, and a split screen for passage and questions, so test day feels familiar.

What is a good IELTS Reading score?

A good score depends on your target. Many universities ask for an overall Band 6.5 or 7 with no section below 6.0 or 6.5, while some migration pathways ask for higher. Always check the exact requirement of your institution or authority.

Are Cambridge IELTS 20 and 21 reading tests available?

Yes. Cambridge IELTS 12 to 21 are available, each with 4 full reading tests, so you can practise the newest Cambridge IELTS reading tests together with older books.

How many questions are in each IELTS Reading passage?

A full test has 40 questions. Passages usually carry about 13 or 14 questions each. On this platform the layout is Passage 1 with questions 1 to 13, Passage 2 with questions 14 to 26 and Passage 3 with questions 27 to 40.

Is there negative marking in IELTS Reading?

No. Wrong answers do not lose marks, so you should never leave a question blank. Make an educated guess if you are unsure.

Should I read the passage or the questions first?

Many candidates skim the passage for structure, then read the questions and scan for keywords. Others read the questions first. Try both methods in mock tests and keep the one that gives you the best accuracy within 20 minutes per passage.

What happens if I run out of time in IELTS Reading?

The test stops after 60 minutes and unanswered questions score zero. Practise the 20-minute-per-passage rhythm, skip time-consuming questions and return to them later, and guess the remaining answers in the last minute.

Can a mock test predict my real IELTS Reading band score?

A mock test gives a useful estimate, but results vary with passage difficulty and your condition on the day. Take several full tests and look at your average score rather than a single result.

Does spelling matter in IELTS Reading answers?

Yes. Spelling must be correct to earn a mark. Both UK and US spellings are accepted, and you can write answers in capital or lower-case letters.

How long should I prepare for IELTS Reading?

It depends on your starting level and target band. Many learners see steady progress with 4 to 8 weeks of regular practice, combining full timed tests, passage-wise practice and careful review of every mistake.
