Free IELTS Reading Mock Test with Authentic Cambridge Papers

Working through past Cambridge papers is one of the most reliable ways to raise your IELTS Reading band. IELTS Mockpoint gives you a free IELTS mock test experience for Reading, using Cambridge IELTS 12 to 19 in an interactive, computer-delivered format.

Take the full Reading test

Sit all 3 passages and 40 questions in one 60-minute session. You get a live timer, highlighting tools and an instant score, so you can see where you stand before the real exam. Remember that IELTS gives no extra time to transfer answers, so every minute counts.

Practise passage by passage

Short on time? Pick Passage 1, Passage 2 or Passage 3 and target your weak spots. Passage 1 is usually the most accessible, while Passage 3 has the most complex argument and the hardest questions.

Question types you will practise

  • Multiple choice and True / False / Not Given
  • Yes / No / Not Given and matching headings
  • Matching information and matching features
  • Sentence, summary, note and table completion
  • Short-answer questions

Start with the newest book and work backwards, or search for a specific test such as "Cambridge 16 test 3". Track the tests you have attempted and repeat weak ones until your accuracy is steady.

Reading format

  • Passage 1 Questions 1–13
  • Passage 2 Questions 14–26
  • Passage 3 Questions 27–40
  • Total questions 40
  • Total duration 60 min
  • Band scale 0 – 9

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our free IELTS Reading mock tests.

Is this IELTS Reading mock test really free?

Yes. You can practise Cambridge IELTS Reading tests online on IELTS Mockpoint and get your score straight after you finish.

How long is the IELTS Reading test?

The IELTS Reading test lasts 60 minutes and has 40 questions across 3 passages. There is no extra time to transfer answers, so managing your time is important.

Can I practise one passage at a time?

Yes. Every test has a Practice mode where you choose Passage 1, Passage 2 or Passage 3 and work on your weak areas without sitting the full test.

Which Cambridge IELTS books are available?

Cambridge IELTS 12 to 19 are available, each with 4 full reading tests.

How is my IELTS Reading band score calculated?

Each correct answer earns one mark out of 40. Your raw score is then converted to a band from 0 to 9. The conversion differs slightly between Academic and General Training.

What question types appear in IELTS Reading?

Common types include multiple choice, True/False/Not Given, Yes/No/Not Given, matching headings, matching information, sentence completion, summary completion and short answer questions.
