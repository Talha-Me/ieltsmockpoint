Most Common IELTS Listening Spelling Traps

In IELTS Listening, a single missing double letter or silent consonant turns a correct answer into zero marks. Master these four high-risk patterns first.

1. Double Letters

Words like accommodation, committee, questionnaire, and occurrence are top mark-losers in Section 1 and Section 3.

2. Silent Letters

Watch silent consonants in Wednesday, February, parliament, receipt, and environment when writing fast.

3. Dictated Names & Codes

Section 1 speakers spell surnames (Whittaker, Mackenzie) and UK postcodes (SW1A 2AA) rapidly—practice with Section 1 Survival mode.

4. Suffix & Vowel Pairs

Keep -ance/-ence (maintenance vs occurrence), -able/-ible, and ie/ei sharp with Trap Spotter mode.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are spelling mistakes penalized in IELTS Listening?

Yes. In IELTS Listening, any misspelled word is marked incorrect (0 points), even if the pronunciation matches. Practicing high-frequency words eliminates these preventable mark losses.

Does IELTS Listening accept both British and American spelling?

Yes, both British (e.g., colour, centre, programme) and American (color, center, program) spellings are accepted across all IELTS Listening sections.

Does capitalization matter in IELTS Listening?

No. You can write answers in lowercase or ALL CAPS, though standard capitalization for days, months, names, and cities is recommended.
