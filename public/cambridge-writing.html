4 quick tips before you start

Plan for 2 minutesA quick outline keeps your ideas organised and your paragraphs focused.
Always write an overviewIn Task 1, state the main trends without going into detail.
Support every ideaIn Task 2, back each main point with a reason or example.
Check your word countStay above the minimum and leave 2 minutes to proofread.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the IELTS Writing test?

60 minutes in total: about 20 minutes for Task 1 (at least 150 words) and 40 minutes for Task 2 (at least 250 words).

Which task carries more marks?

Task 2 counts twice as much as Task 1 in your Writing band score, so give it the extra time.

Can I practise only Task 1 or Task 2?

Yes. Use the Full Test / Task 1 / Task 2 switch above, or the small buttons on each card.

Are these tests free?

Yes, Cambridge Writing practice on IELTS Mockpoint is free. Create an account to save your progress.
