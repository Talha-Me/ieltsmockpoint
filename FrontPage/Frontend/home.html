Common questions

Before you start

Is the IELTS mock test really free?

Yes — you can start a free full mock and a free Cambridge test in every section without paying anything.

Are these Cambridge IELTS tests?

The Listening, Reading and Writing skill tests are Cambridge IELTS practice tests in the real exam interface, so you can practise Cambridge book material online.

How fast do I get my score?

Listening and Reading are graded the moment you submit. Writing and Speaking feedback arrives within 15–25 minutes.

Academic or General Training?

Both are supported across every mock and section test — pick your track before you start.

What is practice by question type?

Instead of a full test, you drill one question type at a time, such as True / False / Not Given or Map Labelling, with 5 practice sets each, until it stops costing you marks.

What is the Practice Hub?

The Practice Hub shows every practice set for Listening, Reading and Writing in one place, so you can jump straight to the one you need.
