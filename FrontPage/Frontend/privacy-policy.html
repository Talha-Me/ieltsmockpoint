Important: This policy is designed to provide clear website privacy and advertising disclosures. The exact information processed depends on which IELTS Mockpoint features you use and which third-party services are active on the website.

1. Introduction

Welcome to IELTS Mockpoint. We provide IELTS preparation resources, practice tests, question-type practice, writing samples, educational content and related online services.

This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored and disclosed when you visit ieltsmockpoint.com, create or use an account, interact with our forms, or use our online practice features.

By using the website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described here, please discontinue use of the website.

2. Information We May Collect

We seek to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for operating, securing and improving the website and its services. Depending on how you use IELTS Mockpoint, this may include:

Account Information

If account creation is available, we may receive information such as your name, email address, login credentials and profile details that you choose to provide.

Practice Information

We may process information related to tests, practice activity, answers, scores, progress and feedback so that requested services can function.

Contact Information

If you contact us, we may receive your email address, message content and other information you voluntarily include in your communication.

Technical Information

Like many websites, the service may receive technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network information, pages visited and general usage data.

Information you provide voluntarily

You may choose to provide information when registering, contacting us, requesting support, participating in features, or submitting feedback. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is specifically required and appropriate for the service.

Information collected automatically

Some information may be collected automatically through server logs, cookies, pixels, analytics tools or similar technologies. This can help us understand website performance, security, traffic patterns and user experience.

3. How We Use Information

Depending on the service you use, information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing, maintaining and improving IELTS Mockpoint services.
  • Creating and managing user accounts where account functionality is offered.
  • Processing practice attempts, scores, progress and requested feedback.
  • Responding to support requests, questions and other communications.
  • Protecting the website against fraud, abuse, unauthorized access and security threats.
  • Understanding website usage and improving content, navigation, performance and reliability.
  • Measuring advertising or marketing performance where applicable.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and enforcing our terms.

We do not intend to use information for purposes that are materially incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected without appropriate notice or a lawful basis where required.

4. Sharing & Third-Party Services

We may use carefully selected third-party service providers to operate parts of the website. Depending on the services currently enabled, these providers may process limited information on our behalf.

Service providers

Providers may support hosting, databases, authentication, email delivery, analytics, security, performance monitoring or other website functions. They should receive only information reasonably necessary for the service they provide.

Advertising partners

If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising providers, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertising subject to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Legal and security disclosures

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, court orders, protect the rights or safety of IELTS Mockpoint or others, investigate abuse, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

We do not sell personal information as a business model. We also do not intentionally provide personal information to advertising partners for purposes unrelated to the disclosed advertising and measurement functions of their services.

5. Cookies, Analytics & Advertising

IELTS Mockpoint may use first-party and third-party cookies or similar technologies. These technologies can help remember preferences, keep services working, understand website traffic, prevent abuse, measure performance and support advertising.

Essential cookies

Some cookies or local-storage technologies may be necessary for login sessions, security, preferences or core website functions. Blocking these technologies may affect parts of the service.

Analytics

Where analytics services are used, they may collect information about how visitors interact with the website, such as pages viewed, approximate geographic information, device/browser characteristics and general traffic patterns. Analytics data is used to understand and improve the website.

Google advertising

If Google AdSense or another Google advertising product is used on IELTS Mockpoint, Google and its partners may use advertising cookies and similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize advertisements. Google states that publishers must clearly disclose these technologies and related data use in their privacy policies.

Users may manage advertising personalization through Google's advertising settings where available. Third-party advertising providers may also offer their own controls for personalized advertising.

Advertising disclosure Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites. Advertising technologies may change as our monetization setup changes. Please review the relevant provider's current privacy and advertising documentation for additional information.

Consent for applicable regions

Where applicable, visitors in regions requiring consent for cookies or personalized advertising may be shown a consent or privacy message. Advertising and consent settings may vary according to the visitor's location and applicable requirements.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You should use a strong, unique password where accounts are offered and avoid sharing your login credentials with others.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining records, resolving disputes, preventing abuse and complying with legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

8. Children's Privacy

IELTS Mockpoint is an educational platform intended for IELTS learners and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action where required.

9. Your Choices & Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information. These may include requesting access, correction, deletion, restriction or information about how your data is processed.

Cookies and advertising choices

You can often control cookies through your browser settings. Where legally required, you may also be presented with a consent management interface for applicable cookies and advertising technologies.

Account information

If you have an account, you may be able to update certain information through your account settings. For additional privacy requests, contact us using the details provided below.

We may need to verify a request before completing it in order to protect the privacy and security of users.

10. International Users

IELTS Mockpoint may use hosting, analytics, advertising and other service providers located in countries different from the country in which you live. Where applicable, information may therefore be processed across borders.

We aim to use appropriate safeguards and follow applicable requirements for international data processing.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services, technology, advertising arrangements or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Your continued use of the website after an update means that you acknowledge the updated policy to the extent permitted by law.

12. Contact Us

If you have a privacy question, request or concern about IELTS Mockpoint, please contact us through the website's contact channel.

When contacting us about a privacy request, please provide enough information for us to understand and verify the request, but do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Common privacy questions

Does IELTS Mockpoint use cookies?

The website may use essential and optional cookies or similar technologies for functionality, analytics, security and advertising, depending on the services enabled on the site.

Does Google AdSense use cookies?

Yes. Browser settings can provide cookie controls, and visitors in certain regions may also receive a consent-management interface where required.

Does IELTS Mockpoint sell my personal information?

IELTS Mockpoint does not use the sale of personal information as a business model. Information may nevertheless be processed by service providers and advertising partners as described in this policy.

How can I make a privacy request?

Use the website's contact page to submit your request. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on a request.
