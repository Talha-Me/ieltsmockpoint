How IELTS Writing Is Scored (Band 6 to Band 9)

Every sample on this page is evaluated across Task Achievement/Response, Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range.

Band 6.0

Addresses the prompt with relevant ideas, though the Task 1 overview may be unclear and occasional grammar slips appear.

Band 7.0

Clear position and logical progression throughout, with minor vocabulary repetition or occasional complex-sentence errors.

Band 8.0

Well-developed arguments, natural topic collocations, and wide grammatical variety with only rare slips.

Band 9.0

Effortless precision, seamless paragraphing, nuanced tone control, and complete coverage of every prompt requirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should IELTS Writing Task 1 and Task 2 answers be?

Task 1 requires at least 150 words (165–190 words is ideal), while Task 2 requires at least 250 words (270–300 words gives enough depth for Band 8–9).

Should I memorise model IELTS essays?

Never memorise whole essays—examiners penalise memorised scripts. Instead, learn the paragraph structure, Task 1 overview phrasing, and Band 8–9 collocations shown in these samples.

Does each writing sample have its own page?

Yes! Clicking any sample opens its dedicated page with the full prompt, paragraph-by-paragraph essay, examiner breakdown, and vocabulary list.
