What should you notice when studying IELTS Writing samples?

The goal is not to copy a model answer. Compare how ideas are selected, developed and connected, then apply the same principles to your own topic.

6

Clear but developing

Ideas are generally relevant, but development, vocabulary range or grammatical control may be inconsistent.

7

Clear and well organized

A stronger response usually has a clear position, logical progression and enough support for the main ideas.

8

Flexible and precise

Look for better control of paragraphing, precise word choice, flexible grammar and well-developed reasoning.

9

Highly controlled

The response is consistently focused, logically organized, fully developed and linguistically natural and accurate.

Writing Task 1

Master the main IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 sample types

Build a flexible report structure instead of memorizing one template. Your overview and comparisons matter more than describing every individual number.

📊 Charts & Graphs

For line graphs, bar charts, pie charts and tables, focus on selecting the key features and grouping similar information logically.

✓Introduce the visual clearly without copying the question.
✓Write a strong overview with the biggest trends or differences.
✓Compare meaningful groups instead of describing data one item at a time.

🗺️ Maps & Processes

For maps and processes, organization is especially important. Show the reader where the description is going and keep the sequence easy to follow.

✓Use accurate location, movement and sequencing language.
✓Group changes or stages into logical paragraphs.
✓Use passive structures naturally when describing processes.

IELTS Writing Task 2

Learn how strong IELTS essay samples are built

Good Task 2 writing is not about using the most complicated words. It is about answering the exact question, developing ideas clearly and controlling your language.

1. Answer the exact question

Identify every part of the prompt before writing. A polished essay can still lose marks if it only answers one part of a two-part question.

2. Make your position clear

Your reader should understand your main position from the introduction and see that position maintained consistently throughout the response.

3. Develop ideas deeply

Do not stop after giving a reason. Explain why it matters, show its consequence and use a relevant example when it genuinely supports your point.

4. Connect ideas naturally

Use paragraph structure, referencing and logical progression to guide the reader. Cohesion should feel natural rather than forced.

5. Use vocabulary precisely

Prioritize accurate, natural vocabulary. A simple word used precisely is better than an advanced word used incorrectly.

6. Control grammar

Mix sentence structures when useful, but prioritize accuracy. Frequent errors can make even strong ideas difficult to understand.

Common questions

IELTS Writing samples — FAQs

Where can I find IELTS Writing samples?

IELTS Mockpoint provides Task 1 and Task 2 model-answer examples organized around common IELTS writing tasks, question types and target band levels.

What is a Band 9 IELTS Writing sample?

A Band 9 sample represents very strong performance across task response or achievement, coherence and cohesion, lexical resource and grammatical range and accuracy. Use it to study principles, not to memorize sentences.

Should I memorize IELTS essay samples?

No. Memorizing complete answers is risky because the actual test question may be different. Instead, learn flexible structures, useful language, idea development and paragraph logic.

What should I study in Task 2 samples?

Pay attention to how the introduction answers the question, how each body paragraph develops one central idea, how examples are used and how the conclusion reflects the argument.

What should I study in Task 1 samples?

Focus on the introduction, overview, selection of key features, comparisons, paragraph grouping and accurate language for describing data, stages or changes.

Can I practise IELTS Writing online?

Yes. After studying a model answer, use IELTS Mockpoint's Writing practice to complete your own Task 1 or Task 2 response under timed conditions.
