Frequently Asked Questions

How long should I speak for an IELTS Speaking Part 2 Cue Card?

You are given 1 minute to prepare brief notes and should aim to speak smoothly for 1.5 to 2 minutes until the examiner asks you to stop.

Should I memorise these Band 9 sample answers word-for-word?

No—examiners immediately spot memorised speeches. Instead, use the structure, natural discourse markers, and Band 9 vocabulary phrases inside your own real stories.

Do these cue card pages include Part 3 follow-up questions?

Yes! Every individual cue card page includes authentic Part 3 discussion questions with Band 8.5–9.0 model answers and an interactive 1-min / 2-min practice timer.
