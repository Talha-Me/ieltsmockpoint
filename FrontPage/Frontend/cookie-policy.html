2. What Are Cookies?

A cookie is a small text file that a website may store in your browser or on your device. When you return to a website, the browser can send relevant information back to the website.

Cookies can be temporary (“session” cookies) or remain for a period of time (“persistent” cookies). Other technologies, such as local storage, can perform similar functions without being traditional cookies.

Why do websites use them?

They can help remember a login session, maintain preferences, protect forms, measure page performance, understand website usage and support features that would otherwise be difficult to provide.

4. Essential Technologies

Essential cookies or similar technologies may be necessary for certain features to function. For example, they may help with login sessions, account security, form protection, test-session functionality or basic website operation.

Blocking essential technologies can cause parts of the website to stop working correctly. For example, account login, mock-test sessions or other interactive features may not function as intended.

5. Functional Technologies

Functional technologies may remember choices you make while using IELTS Mockpoint. Depending on the features available, these may include language or interface preferences, previously selected settings or other user-experience choices.

Their purpose is generally to make repeated visits easier and reduce the need to enter or select the same preferences repeatedly.

6. Analytics & Performance Technologies

Depending on the analytics service used, information may be collected by a third-party provider under its own privacy and cookie practices.

Analytics are not the same as an official IELTS assessment.

Analytics measure website usage and technical performance. They do not measure your IELTS ability, determine an official band score or replace official IELTS assessment.

10. Your Cookie Choices

Most modern browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings. You may be able to block cookies, delete existing cookies, receive a notification before a cookie is stored or use private browsing features.

The exact controls vary by browser and device. If you disable cookies, some website features may not work properly.

Browser controls

  • Review your browser's privacy or cookie settings.
  • Delete stored cookies when you choose.
  • Block third-party cookies where your browser provides that option.
  • Use browser privacy or private-browsing modes where appropriate.

Where a cookie or consent preference interface is provided on the website, you may also use the available controls there.

11. Local Storage & Similar Browser Technologies

Some IELTS Mockpoint features may use browser local storage or similar technologies instead of traditional cookies. These technologies can store information directly in the browser and may be used to support interactive features or remember temporary user preferences.

Depending on the browser, deleting site data, clearing storage or using private browsing may remove information stored by these technologies.

12. How Long Technologies May Remain

The lifetime of a cookie or similar technology can vary. Session technologies may disappear when a browsing session ends, while persistent technologies may remain until they expire or are deleted.

The retention period can depend on the purpose of the technology, the website feature involved, browser settings and the third-party provider that sets it.

What are cookies?

Does IELTS Mockpoint use cookies?

IELTS Mockpoint may use cookies and similar technologies depending on the features and services enabled on the platform. These may support essential functions, preferences, analytics and advertising.

Can I disable cookies?

Are advertising cookies used?

Are cookies used to calculate my official IELTS band score?
