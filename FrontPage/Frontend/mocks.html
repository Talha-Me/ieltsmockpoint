Academic IELTS mock tests

Choose Academic if you plan to study at a university or apply for professional registration. Academic Reading uses long, university-level passages, and Writing Task 1 asks you to describe a graph, chart, table or diagram. Our Academic mocks include every common question type, from True / False / Not Given to matching headings and summary completion.

General Training IELTS mock tests

General Training is for migration, work experience and secondary education. Reading uses everyday and workplace texts, and Writing Task 1 is a letter. Listening and Speaking are the same as Academic, so your preparation for those skills carries across.

Premium Academic IELTS mock tests

Premium Academic mocks are our closest match to the real test. They are ideal for the last weeks of preparation, when you want to check your readiness, build stamina for the full 2 hour 45 minute session and practise your exam-day routine.

Free IELTS mock test: questions, answered

Are the IELTS mock tests on IELTS Mockpoint free?

Yes. All full-length mock tests and individual skill tests are currently free to take. You do not need a Student ID or passcode.

Where can I take a free full IELTS mock test online?

You can take a free full IELTS mock test on IELTS Mockpoint. Choose Academic, General Training or Premium Academic, press Start Test, and sit all sections in the real exam interface with real timing.

What is the difference between General and Academic IELTS mocks?

Listening and Speaking are the same. Academic Reading and Writing use university-level texts and data-based tasks, while General Training uses everyday and workplace texts and a letter for Task 1.

How long is a full IELTS mock test?

About 2 hours 45 minutes: Listening 30 minutes, Reading 60 minutes, Writing 60 minutes and Speaking 11 to 14 minutes.

What are Premium Academic IELTS mock tests?

Premium Academic mocks are our most exam-like tests, designed to match the real interface and difficulty so you can check your readiness before test day.

How many mock tests should I take before the real exam?

Most candidates benefit from five or more full mocks, reviewing mistakes after each one and practising weak skills separately in between.

Do I need to sign up or log in to start a mock test?

No. All full mock tests are free right now and you can start without a login or sign-up. Creating a free account lets you save and track your results.

How fast do I get my IELTS mock test score?

Listening and Reading are graded the moment you submit. Writing and Speaking feedback arrives within 15 to 25 minutes.

Which IELTS mock test should I start with?

Start with Academic Mock Test 1 or General Mock Test 1 as a diagnostic, then move on to the Premium Academic mocks in the last weeks before your exam.

Is an IELTS mock test score the same as my real IELTS score?

A mock test gives you a reliable estimate of your level, but only the official IELTS test report from the test centre is your real score. Use mock results to find weak areas and track progress.

What is a good IELTS band score?

It depends on your goal. Many universities ask for an overall band between 6.0 and 7.0, while some courses and visa routes need higher. Always check the requirement of your institution or immigration authority.

Can I practise IELTS Listening, Reading, Writing or Speaking separately?

Yes. Besides full mocks, IELTS Mockpoint has free Cambridge IELTS Listening, Reading and Writing tests, a Speaking mock test and a Practice Hub with practice sets for every question type.
