Exam format

Computer-delivered IELTS format: 2h 45m simulation

Exactly what you will face on test day. Every module is timed to official test standards.

Module 1

Listening

⏱ 30 minutes + 2 mins review

4 recorded sections, 40 questions total. Conversations, academic discussions and lectures with native British, Australian and North American accents.

Module 2

Reading

⏱ 60 minutes

3 authentic long passages, 40 questions total. Split-screen layout matching the official computer-delivered test environment.

Module 3

Writing

⏱ 60 minutes

Task 1 (150 words) and Task 2 (250 words). Built-in live word counter, split-screen prompt viewing, and exam-standard text editor.

Module 4

Speaking

⏱ 11–14 minutes

Part 1, Part 2 cue cards, and Part 3 abstract discussion. Full voice simulation powered by real IELTS examiner criteria.
