3 quick tips before you press play

Read firstUse the pause before each section to read the questions and predict the type of answer.
Watch spellingA misspelt or over-length answer loses the mark, so check words and word limits.
Follow signpostsWords like "however" and "actually" often signal the answer is about to change.
